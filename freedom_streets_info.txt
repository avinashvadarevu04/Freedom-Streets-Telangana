Executive Summary: A Vision for Healthier Communities
Freedom Streets Telangana 2026 presents a transformative opportunity to address critical public health challenges within our urban centers, fostering a 
more connected and vibrant society.
The Challenge: Urban Health 
Crisis
Telangana faces escalating public health 
concerns, including a significant rise in 
mental health issues, driven by urban 
stress and social fragmentation. 
Concurrently, sedentary lifestyles 
contribute to a growing burden of non-
communicable diseases. These factors 
collectively strain healthcare resources and 
diminish overall quality of life, costing the 
state millions annually in direct and indirect 
expenditures.
Our Solution: Scalable Public 
Health Intervention
Freedom Streets Telangana is a proven, 
community-led intervention that reclaims 
public spaces for health and social 
interaction. By closing streets to traffic 
periodically, we create safe environments 
for physical activity, community 
engagement, and cultural celebrations. This 
scalable model directly combats sedentary 
behavior, reduces urban isolation, and 
enhances mental well-being through 
accessible, free programming.
The Ask: Partnership for Impact
We seek the invaluable support and 
strategic partnership of the Telangana 
Government to expand Freedom Streets 
across key urban districts. With an 
investment of 
¹2.7-3 Crores, we can scale 
our impact, directly benefiting over 4 crore 
citizens of Telangana by 2027. This 
collaboration represents a direct 
investment in preventative healthcare, 
social cohesion, and the long-term well-
being of our state, yielding significant 
returns in public health and economic 
productivity.

Implementation & Government Partnership
The success of Freedom Streets Telangana hinges on a robust, collaborative partnership with key government agencies. This initiative is designed as a government-led program, ensuring seamless 
integration with urban planning and public health mandates, maximizing reach and long-term sustainability.
Government Agency Involvement
GHMC
Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation
Coordination for public space allocation, event permits, and infrastructure support to facilitate street closures and event setups.
DISTRICT ADMINISTRATION
Local District Authorities
Liaison for ground-level execution, community mobilization, and ensuring smooth operations across various districts within Phase 1.
HEAL TH DEPARTMENT
Public Health & Wellness
Integration of health advisories, medical support, and public awareness campaigns to promote the program's health benefits and ensure safety protocols.
TRAFFIC POLICE
Traffic Management & Safety
Ensuring efficient traffic diversions, public safety, and crowd control during event hours, making designated streets safe for participants.
Governance & Oversight
A dedicated Joint Working Group (JWG) comprising representatives from Freedom Streets and key government departments will be established to oversee program execution.
Monthly review meetings will be held to monitor progress, address operational challenges, and ensure alignment with state objectives.
All program reports, impact assessments, and financial statements will be regularly submitted to the Telangana Government for transparency and accountability.

Success Metrics & Accountability
Our commitment to the Telangana Government is underpinned by clear, measurable outcomes and transparent reporting. 
We define success through the following key indicators:
Participant Reach
Track total unique participants per session and overall program engagement.
Mental Health Index
Measure pre- and post-program changes in participants' well-being and stress levels via surveys.
Community Engagement Scores
Assess social cohesion and sense of belonging through feedback and attendance data.
Cost-Effectiveness Ratio
Evaluate the program's impact on public health per rupee invested, demonstrating ROI.
Media & Public Perception
Monitor positive media mentions, social media sentiment, and public awareness of the initiative.
State Health Target Alignment
Demonstrate direct contributions to the state's public health objectives and wellness goals.
Progress against these metrics will be rigorously tracked and reported to government stakeholders on a quarterly basis, 
ensuring full transparency and adaptive program management.

Scaling & Long-term Vision
Phase 1 is just the beginning. Our strategic roadmap outlines a clear path for expanding Freedom Streets across 
Telangana and beyond, transforming it into a flagship government initiative and a model for national wellness.
1
Phase 1: Foundation (March-June 2026)
Establish core operations in GHMC zones (Ranga Reddy, Medchal-Malkajgiri, Sangareddy). Gather 
critical data, refine programming, and build local leadership to ensure a robust, replicable model.
2
Phase 2: Statewide Expansion (July 2026 - June 2027)
Expand to all 33 districts of Telangana, engaging an estimated 
4 crore (40 million) citizens. Diversify 
activities based on regional needs, integrating local cultural elements to foster deeper community 
ownership.
3
Phase 3: National Replication (2027 Onwards)
Position Freedom Streets as a proven model for public health intervention, ready for replication across 
other Indian states. Project long-term economic benefits from reduced healthcare burdens and 
increased productivity, alongside enhanced social cohesion and mental well-being for millions.
This phased approach ensures sustainable growth, maximizing impact and cementing Freedom Streets as a 
beacon of community-led wellness for the nation.

The event setup for Freedom Streets on Dallas Road, Hyderabad, is meticulously designed to optimize the use of urban space for diverse community 
activities. With a central Main Stage for high-energy fitness sessions, dedicated tracks for running and hurdles, and specific zones for pets and children, 
every segment caters to a unique interest. Strategically placed food and merchandise stalls, along with ample seating areas, ensure convenience and 
engagement, while clearly marked entry and exit points prioritize safety and efficient crowd flow. This organized approach transforms the road into a 
vibrant hub for health and community interaction.
